# elppA TV

An Android TV home screen for Anjan's Sony KD-50X75: cinematic recommended-show artwork, an app dock and a horizontal Continue Watching / Play Next shelf. The remote is the primary input, viewed from a sofa. Keep stock Google voice search, phone remote control and Sony settings.

Phase 1 uses accessible app-published TV content; coverage varies by TV/app. No claim of Google's personalised feed or universal OTT watch history. The source fork keeps CleanYourTV's reference app intact and adds a separate native app module. See docs/elppa/PHASE-1.md.
