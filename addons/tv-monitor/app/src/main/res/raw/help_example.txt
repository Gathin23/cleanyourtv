1) python tv_ai_agent.py --setup
   saves, encrypted, in %APPDATA%\tv-monitor\config.json:
   {"url": "https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages",
    "model": "claude-sonnet-5-5",
    "key": "dpapi:...",
    "host": "192.168.1.50",
    "revert_seconds": 60}

2) python tv_ai_agent.py 192.168.1.50 --apply --watch

3) What the PC sends when you press Ask:
   POST https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages
   x-api-key: <your key>
   anthropic-version: 2023-06-01
   content-type: application/json

   {"model": "claude-sonnet-5-5",
    "max_tokens": 1500,
    "system": "You help keep an Android TV healthy...",
    "messages": [{"role": "user",
                  "content": "<snapshot of the TV, as JSON>"}]}

4) What it expects back (in content[0].text):
   {"summary": "one or two sentences",
    "findings": ["short observation"],
    "actions": [{"type": "force_stop",
                 "package": "com.example.app",
                 "risk": "low",
                 "reason": "why"}]}

To see exactly what is sent, without sending it:
   python tv_ai_agent.py 192.168.1.50 --show-payload
