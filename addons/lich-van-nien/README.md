# Lịch Vạn Niên (Vietnamese almanac)

An optional, standalone module card for the Vietnamese lunar almanac. The Home tile says whether today is a
*hoàng đạo* (auspicious) or *hắc đạo* (inauspicious) day and lists today's six good double-hours, so a glance
at Home is enough. OK opens a full screen with the lunar date, the *can chi* (sexagenary names) of day, month
and year, the day's star, a 12-hour ring with the zodiac animals (the TV's own emoji font) that marks the good
hours and the current one, and the day's *trực* with what it suits and what to avoid. Left/Right page through
days, OK returns to today. Everything is computed on the TV for Vietnam (UTC+7); no data is downloaded.
Design notes in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). The screen is in Vietnamese.

## How the tile changes every day

A card can only carry fixed text and a fixed picture, so the app ships 12 launcher aliases (`.Day0` … `.Day11`),
each with its own banner: 6 sets of good hours × good/bad day. `DayUpdater` keeps the one for today enabled
and the others disabled: after boot, after a clock or time-zone change, on an inexact alarm within 15 minutes
after midnight (Vietnam time) and whenever the screen is opened. The launcher rebuilds its tiles on
`PACKAGE_CHANGED`. Because the tile's component changes with the day, a position chosen with the tile menu's
Move does not stick; put the package in `dock_apps` (matched by package name) to pin it.

## Setup

Build from this folder with JDK 17 and Android SDK 34:

```sh
./gradlew --no-daemon testReleaseUnitTest assembleRelease
```

Sideload `app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk` with the repository's normal ADB tools, then enable
today's tile once (the app has no launcher entry until it has run):

```sh
TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/venv/bin/python -I tools/tv.py 'am broadcast -n com.tungpv.lichvannien/.DayReceiver'
```

Press Home and approve **Lich Van Nien** when the launcher asks about its card. Until the app has run once the
tile shows a neutral "Hôm nay / Bấm OK" picture. If the app is ever force-stopped, Android drops its alarm and its
boot receiver until it is opened again, so the tile can stay on an old day: open the tile once.

Preview another day's tile from ADB only (`PreviewReceiver` is protected by `DUMP`, which apps cannot hold):
`am broadcast -n com.tungpv.lichvannien/.PreviewReceiver --ei preview_days 1`; `--ei preview_days 0`, or the
next midnight, puts today's back. The banners are generated by `tools/make_banners.py` (Pillow, fonts pulled from
the TV's `/system/fonts`).

## Data and validation

The lunar calendar is Hồ Ngọc Đức's algorithm (astronomical new moons and solar terms at UTC+7). The day's
star, *trực* and good hours follow the usual Vietnamese almanac tables (*hoàng đạo* by the day's branch within
the lunar month, *trực* by the month's branch, good hours by the day's branch). Unit tests compare 9 dates in
2025–2027 with xemlicham.com and ngaydep.com (lunar date, can chi, star, trực, good hours), plus two 2027 solar
terms that begin just after midnight. From 23:00 the ring's Tý segment follows the next day, since giờ Tý opens
the next day. Left/Right stop at 1900 and 2199.

## Permissions

- `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`: to put today's tile back after the TV starts.

No network access, no background service, no accessibility service. One inexact daily alarm that does not wake
the TV.
