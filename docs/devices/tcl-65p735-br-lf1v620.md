# TCL 65P735 (Brazil), LF1V620

Tested on a Brazilian TCL 65P735 sold as Smart TV Pro, device BeyondTV4, Android TV 11
(API 30), firmware `V8-R51MT05-LF1V620`, security patch `2024-03-05`.
See also the [TCL G03 example](../../examples/tcl-g03-google-tv/) for related units.

- The launcher ran as a regular TV app. Streaming apps and the Play Store opened.
- Setting the custom Home with ADB returned `Success`, but Home resolution and an
  injected Home press both still went to `com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx/.home.HomeActivity`.
- With the stock launcher enabled, `stock_home_redirect` worked after the launcher's
  accessibility service bound. Initially TCL reported `APP_AUTO_START: ignore`
  and the service failed to bind. Allowing `APP_AUTO_START` for the launcher and
  re-enabling the service made the redirect work on an injected Home press.
  The original Home could briefly appear before the redirect.
- After a full reboot the service was bound and the autostart setting persisted,
  but the first settled screen observed was the stock Home.

This was tested on a local CleanYourTV derivative. The unmodified upstream APK,
automatic redirection at boot, standby wake, Alexa and long-press volume were
not verified. No debloat list was applied.
