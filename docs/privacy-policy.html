1. Introduction

Norma Launcher ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a cinematic, high-performance home screen replacement for Android TV devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is accessed, how it is handled, and confirms that no personal information is collected or transmitted outside your local device.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Norma Launcher adheres to a strict offline-first, privacy-by-design model. We do not maintain any servers, databases, or user accounts.

A. Installed Applications

The launcher accesses the list of applications installed on your TV via Android's built-in PackageManager API. This is necessary solely to display your apps on the home screen dock and 5×5 app drawer, and to allow you to launch, reorder, or organize them. This data never leaves your TV.

B. Location Data (Weather & Timezone)

Norma Launcher requests location permissions exclusively to provide accurate real-time local weather forecasts (temperature and conditions) and to keep the clock synchronized to your local timezone. Location coordinates are used only on-device to query open, public meteorological APIs (e.g., Open-Meteo). We do not record, store, or profile your location history.

C. Local Storage & File Access

When you customize wallpapers from internal storage, the built-in file browser reads image files located on your TV storage. All image processing and caching are stored locally within the app's private sandbox directory.

D. Device Preferences & Audio

Settings such as sound effect toggles, wallpaper themes, screensaver modes (Countdown Solat or Clocks), and dock layout preferences are stored strictly in private Android SharedPreferences.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Norma Launcher is 100% ad-free. We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, commercial tracking mechanisms, or behavioral analytics tools.

4. Children’s Privacy

Norma Launcher does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is family-friendly and safe for all TV users.

5. Security

Because all data remains strictly confined to your TV, your settings and personal preferences are protected by Android's internal operating system sandbox security architecture.

6. Contact & Open Source Repository

For inquiries, feedback, or bug reports regarding Norma Launcher, please visit our GitHub repository issues tracker.
