# Stock launcher: com.google.android.tvlauncher (the restore.sh default is correct).
# The only other HOME claimant is com.android.tv.settings/.system.FallbackHome, so even a
# failed launcher takeover still lands on a settings home rather than a black screen.
# Live TV / tuner app: com.sony.dtv.tvx (set it as live_tv_package in the launcher's config).
# Keep the com.sony.dtv.tvinput.* services, com.sony.dtv.braviasync*, com.sony.dtv.tvapi,
# com.sony.dtv.sonysystemservice and the Sony settings apps: they drive HDMI, the tuner,
# Bravia Sync and the remote. Also keep com.sony.dtv.airplayapp and com.sony.dtv.homekit
# (AirPlay and HomeKit), and the update services (com.sony.dtv.update, .systemupdate,
# .oadservice) if you want OTA firmware updates. Left enabled by choice here:
# com.sony.dtv.seeds.iot* , the Vewd core and com.youview.poa / com.sony.dtv.youview.
# Already disabled at the factory: com.sony.dtv.b2b.vendorprotocol, com.sony.dtv.b2b.softap,
# screnmirroring.com (that typo'd name is exactly what Sony ships — do not "fix" it).
# On the Taiwan build (BRAVIA_UR3_TW, KD-55X8000H), com.sony.dtv.promos is not installed, and
# com.sony.dtv.seeds.iot, com.vewd.core.integration.dia, com.google.android.videos and
# com.google.android.play.games ship disabled at the factory.
#
# Applied in one session and reboot-checked: all 16 below stayed disabled after a reboot on EU,
# and the tracking/ad/demo subset plus com.google.android.tv.bugreportsender and the reference
# launcher (com.gathin.tvhome) stayed disabled/default across a reboot on the Taiwan KD-55X8000H
# (with voice assistant, Chromecast, AirPlay/HomeKit, Miracast and streaming apps kept by choice).
# KD-65A8 (#2717): all 18 below were present and disabled cleanly, applied in batches on 2026-10-30
# and reboot-checked twice; used for one evening so far. The reference launcher took Home through
# launcher/build-and-install.sh and the real remote worked with no key helper (Home, Input, gear,
# Netflix/YouTube/Prime Video buttons, held volume, power-on). Wake the TV first if the Backdrop

# Packages disabled seconds before `adb reboot` can come back on; wait about 90 s before rebooting.
