# TCL Smart TV Pro (G08, G08_4K_GB, MediaTek), Australian firmware, Android 14 (Google TV)
# Model "Smart TV Pro", device G08, platform merak, fingerprint TCL/G08_4K_GB/G08:14/UTT2.250416.001/AU02:user/release-keys,
# 32-bit userspace (armeabi-v7a).
# The same model as examples/tcl-g08-au-android12, after TCL's Android 14 update. New on this firmware: Android's ad
# and personalisation services, TCL's Alexa client, a TCL account, the Home push service tclhome_passive and the
# message box. The voice assistant (katniss) and Google Cast (mediashell) are present here and were kept.
#
# Tested on one set from 30 October 2026, lived with for two weeks with nothing broken or re-enabled. Applied with
# tools/disable.sh in four batches with a screenshot after each, then the launcher with launcher/build-and-install.sh
# (no key helper). Checked afterwards: the remote's Input key panel, HDMI 1 and HDMI 2, the app and Settings
# buttons on the real remote, the streaming apps, Private DNS on AdGuard. Not checked: Live TV (no aerial),
# Chromecast, AirPlay and Miracast sessions.
#
# Apply:   TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/disable.sh examples/tcl-g08-au-android14/debloat.txt
# Undo:    TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/restore.sh examples/tcl-g08-au-android14/debloat.txt com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled. Comment out any line you want to keep.
#
# Stock launcher: com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx. Live TV / HDMI app: com.tcl.tv.
# Left enabled on purpose: com.tcl.tv, com.tcl.tvinput, com.tcl.systemserver, com.tcl.ui_mediaCenter (USB media
# player), com.tcl.partnercustomizer (its "TCL Channel" tile appears in the app grid once launcherx is off; hide it
# with hidden_apps), the firmware updater, the keyboards, Play Store and Play Services, plus the DO NOT DISABLE
# lines below.
# Kept by the owner's choice: every streaming app, the voice assistant (com.google.android.katniss), Google Cast
# (com.google.android.apps.mediashell), AirPlay (com.tcl.airplay2, com.mediatek.AirplayAPK,
# com.mediatek.airplaydaemon), screen mirroring (com.tcl.miracast), TCL Live free channels (com.tcl.channelplus)
# and TCL Channel (com.tcl.waterfall.overseas): with no aerial they are the only live channels.
