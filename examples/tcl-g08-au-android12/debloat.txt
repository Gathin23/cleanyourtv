# TCL Smart TV Pro (G08, G08_4K_GB, MediaTek), Australian firmware, Android 12 (Google TV, Basic TV mode)
# Model "Smart TV Pro", device G08, fingerprint TCL/G08_4K_GB/G08:12/STT2.221228.001/AS34:user/release-keys,
# 32-bit userspace (armeabi-v7a), about 2.4 GB RAM.
# Same fingerprint as examples/tcl-g08-eu-android12, but the Australian set also ships Stan, AirPlay
# (com.tcl.airplay2 + com.mediatek.AirplayAPK), TCL Channel (com.tcl.waterfall.overseas), TCL Live free
# channels (com.tcl.channelplus), com.tcl.esticker, com.tcl.hotelmenu and com.tcl.guard, and has no Google
# Cast (mediashell) and no voice assistant (katniss). com.tcl.browser, com.tcl.messagebox,
# com.tcl.smartalexa and com.google.android.videos from the EU list are not on it.
#
# Tested 22 October 2026 on one set, applied with tools/disable.sh in five batches with screenshots between
# them. All 28 packages stayed disabled across a reboot (pm list packages -d compared before and after);
# the Source key panel, HDMI 1/2 switching, SmartTube and Plex were checked afterwards (AirPlay and Miracast
# sessions were not), and the launcher was installed with launcher/build-and-install.ps1 -HomeKeyService
# (HOME_KEY_SERVICE=1 launcher/build-and-install.sh on macOS or Linux).
#
# Apply:   TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/disable.sh examples/tcl-g08-au-android12/debloat.txt
# Undo:    TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/restore.sh examples/tcl-g08-au-android12/debloat.txt com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled. Comment out any line you want to keep.
#
# Stock launcher: com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx. Live TV / HDMI app: com.tcl.tv.
# Already disabled before this list on the tested set, by Google TV Basic TV mode: com.netflix.ninja,
# com.google.android.youtube.tv, au.com.stan.and (the recovery record keeps them disabled on undo).
# Left enabled on purpose: com.tcl.tv, com.tcl.tvinput, com.tcl.systemserver, com.tcl.ui_mediaCenter (USB
# media player), com.tcl.partnercustomizer (its TKeyFree "TCL Channel" tile shows up as an app once launcherx
# is off; hide it with hidden_apps instead), com.tcl.useragreement, tv.mopa.ginga, the keyboards,
# Play Store and Play Services, plus the DO NOT DISABLE lines below.
# Kept by the owner's choice: AirPlay (com.tcl.airplay2, com.mediatek.AirplayAPK, com.mediatek.airplaydaemon -
# the EU list disables the daemon, AirPlay needs it) and screen mirroring (com.tcl.miracast).
