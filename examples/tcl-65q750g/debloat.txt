# com.tcl.keyhelp, com.tcl.autopair, com.tcl.tcl_bt_rcu_service (remote), com.tcl.providers.config,

# in TCL's own privacy settings instead.

# Tested 2026-11-03 on a real unit, in four batches with a screenshot after each; Home, HDMI inputs (seven
# com.tcl.tvinput inputs still registered) and the remote kept working.
# Reboot check: com.tcl.usercenter and com.tcl.smartalexa come back at every boot (as on the G05 list);
# everything else stayed disabled.
# setupwraith on AR62: Wi-Fi belonged to com.android.tv.settings (`dumpsys wifi | grep -o 'cname=[^ ]*'`),
# so disabling setupwraith kept the TV on Wi-Fi. It stayed disabled across two reboots, like AR71 and unlike
# AR68. Order: this list (setupwraith included) while launcherx still owned Home, a reboot, then
# launcher/build-and-install.sh. Home stayed com.gathin.tvhome after a reboot, and the TV powers on to Home.
# App buttons (Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video), Input (Quick Panel, com.tcl.suspension kept), Settings, Home
# and the mic all worked on the real remote with launcherx off, without INPUT_KEY_SERVICE or HOME_KEY_SERVICE.
# Private DNS (dns.adguard-dns.com) validated and YouTube loaded with it.
