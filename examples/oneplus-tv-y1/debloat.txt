# Y1 43": disabled on one TV on 2026-10-18 and lived with through 2026-10-20 without trouble; checked with

# switching, Bluetooth voice remote, audio, and launcher/ installed as Home). Unlike the Panasonic list on the

# days without side effects.

# TalkBack was already disabled before the Y1 43" debloat; it is not in debloat.txt.
