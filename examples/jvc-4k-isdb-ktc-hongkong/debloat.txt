# two tuners; CEC reports four HDMI ports with ARC on HDMI 1).

# Applied by hand on one set on 2026-11-13 with `pm disable-user --user 0`, one package per call (not through
# tools/disable.sh), then one reboot: the three packages below stayed disabled. Lived with every day until
# 2026-11-24 (11 days), launcher on the Home button, with nothing breaking; several cold boots (unplugged from
# the mains) in that time, after each of which the TV came back working (owner's report, not read over ADB).
#
# Switched back on at every boot by this firmware (cause not identified; lastDisabledCaller empty, and the
# boot log shows both being re-read while system_server starts, before any app runs). Both were disabled and
# checked, and both were enabled again after the reboot, so they are commented out below rather than disabled
# again after each boot. With katniss disabled, the three voice settings (voice_interaction_service,
# assistant, voice_recognition_service) went empty until the reboot put them back.
#
# Kept on purpose: ktc.factorymenu.ui (carries the MediaTek HotKeyService, VolService and the GLOBAL_BUTTON
#   receivers - never launched), fusion.android.tv.demo (declares the TV_INPUT_BUTTON activities - never
#   launched), com.mediatek.autopair (remote pairing), com.mediatek.network, com.mediatek.wwtv.mediaplayer
#   (USB player), Dolby audio, Gboard, Play Store and Play Services (system updates come through them; no
#   vendor updater on this build), Chromecast built-in, com.google.android.tv.remote.service (phone remote),
#   launcherx and setupwraith (see above; setupwraith also carries a CastInfoProvider here).
# Not touched: com.ktc.serialport, com.ktc.tv.factory, mediatek.factorymenu.ui (factory tools, never started),
#   and the Google partner/one-time/auto-install/data-saver packages the Philips list disables
#   (partnersetup, onetimeinitializer, gtvpai, smartconnect.tv, partnercustomizer): not tried with launcherx
#   still on Home.
#
# The saved Wi-Fi networks belong to android.uid.system, not to setupwraith (dumpsys wifi), and the set was on
# its built-in Ethernet with Wi-Fi off. A dormant managed profile (UserInfo{10:new_user}) exists from an older
# firmware; it was left alone, and nothing here needed launcherx disabled, so the second-user crash in
# docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md does not come into play.
