# OnePlus TV (model C00, codename Cebu), Android TV 10 (API 29), firmware QTG3.201207.001
# MediaTek m7632, armeabi-v7a, UI at 1920x1080, ~1.8 GB RAM, India. Classic Android TV home, not Google TV.
# Stock launcher: com.google.android.tvlauncher. Live TV app: com.heytap.tv.livetv.
#
# Apply:   TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/disable.sh examples/oneplus-tv-cebu/debloat.txt
# Undo:    TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/restore.sh examples/oneplus-tv-cebu/debloat.txt
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled. Comment out any line you want to keep.
#
# Kept on purpose: the HDMI/tuner stack (com.mediatek.tvinput, com.mediatek.tv.agent, com.heytap.tv.support,
# com.mediatek.tvinputservice.arbitratorservice, com.heytap.tvapiservice), Live TV (com.heytap.tv.livetv),
# the remote service and TV keyboard (com.oneplus.tv.remote), the hot keys (com.mediatek.hotkey.dispatcher,
# which handles the Input key), the Netflix key (com.mstar.netflixobserver), Google Assistant
# (com.google.android.katniss), firmware updates (com.oneplus.tv.systemupgrade, com.heytap.oprcupgradeservice),
# Play Store and Play Services. Left alone and never launched: the factory apps (com.heytap.optvfactory,
# com.oneplus.tv.factory). setupwraith does not claim HOME here.
# With launcher/ as Home (live_tv_package com.heytap.tv.livetv), Home, Input, Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video
# and the Assistant mic on the remote work without InputKeyService or HomeKeyService.
# USB debugging alone opens port 5555, and it stays open across reboots until switched off.
# Reboot-checked: nothing came back enabled. AdGuard Private DNS validates.
