# Quirks on this firmware:
# - com.tcl.usercenter comes back enabled at every boot, even with its Play update removed (TCL's boot-time list;

#   does the same, see group 3.
# - com.tcl.partnercustomizer also supplies the stock Android TV Home's Inputs icon (and the TCL Channel tile and a
#   TV Guard shortcut). Disabling it removes the icon; the remote's Source button still works. No effect with a
#   custom launcher.
# - Disabling com.google.android.katniss (group 6) also disables search on the stock home screen, typed search too.
# - Settings > Applications opens TCL "TV Guard" (com.tcl.tvweishi), and still does while it is disabled.
# - Re-enable com.tcl.tvweishi and com.tcl.appmarket2 (group 7) while installing the launcher, as on the RT2851 list.
# - 55C718: disable.sh skipped android.autoinstalls.config.tcl.device and com.google.android.leanbacklauncher.recommendations
