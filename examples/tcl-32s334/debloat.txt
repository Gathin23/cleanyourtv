# TCL 32S334 (Android product BeyondTV5, device BeyondTV; North American variant, Realtek RTD2851 / rtd285o),
# Android TV 9 (API 28), build PPR1.180610.011 AP13, security patch 2020-11-01.
# Tested on two identical units. The owner reports several days of use without issues; CleanYourTV Home
# and the receiver add-ons were still running after a reboot. The retail model number was supplied by the owner.
#
# Apply: TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/disable.sh examples/tcl-32s334/debloat.txt
# Undo:  TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/restore.sh examples/tcl-32s334/debloat.txt
# Stock launcher: com.google.android.tvlauncher; disable only after CleanYourTV is confirmed as Home.
# Live TV app: com.tcl.tv. Leave the tuner, HDMI passthrough, source menu, remote, Bluetooth,
# keyboard, Play Store, and Play Services packages enabled.
# Already disabled before setup on both units: com.tcl.bootadservice and uk.co.freeview.systemdistributor.
# On TV 2, Play Music, Play Games, and Play Movies & TV were also disabled before setup; they were
# re-enabled to match TV 1 and are not part of this list.
# Chromecast and firmware updates were kept. Miracast was already off in Settings on both units.
# com.tcl.smartalexa was disabled before the first reboot, but TCL re-enabled it at boot on both units;
# it was left enabled after reboot and is intentionally not listed as an action here.
