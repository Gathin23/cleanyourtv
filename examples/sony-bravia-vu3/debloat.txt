# Second unit: KD-55X75K, India, same build (UKR1.240726.001). Every line applied unchanged, in three
# batches (setupwraith before launcherx); the same packages were still disabled after a reboot, and power-on
# goes through com.realtek.advancesetting/.FallbackHome to the new launcher, not to Live TV. Only
# com.sony.dtv.b2b.vendorprotocol was disabled at the factory here. Port 5555 was closed: Wireless debugging
# pairing, then `adb tcpip 5555`. The saved Wi-Fi belonged to android.uid.system, so setupwraith was safe to
# disable. Once launcherx is off, the remote's Netflix, YouTube, YouTube Music and Prime Video buttons do
# nothing; Input, the settings button, the mic and Home keep working without a helper. Those four buttons are
# vendor keys told apart by scancode (getevent EV_KEY 0x2f6, 0x239, 0x28d, 0x28c), so config.json maps them as
# scan:758, scan:569, scan:653 and scan:652 (INPUT_KEY_SERVICE=1). With that helper bound, holding volume
