# The built-in Wi-Fi is damaged (water), so all work ran over built-in Ethernet.

# the firmware opens com.tcl.tv a couple of seconds later. The owner chose to accept
# that and press Home. The launcher theme hides the TCL and Alexa tiles. See README.md.
