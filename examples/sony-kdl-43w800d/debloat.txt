# *** DO NOT DISABLE THE com.sony.dtv.b2b.* HOTEL PACKAGES ON THIS PLATFORM. ***
# They were in an earlier version of this list and broke Sony's Settings app: Settings opened and
# closed again within a second, its window never taking focus. logcat showed the Settings activity
# logging "onPause view Null" straight after "Setting dim to 0.7", while com.sony.dtv.configsettings
# repeated "Failed to find provider info for com.sony.dtv.b2b.hotelsettings" and
# "HotelSettings: Can't get key: hotel_mode". Sony's Settings reads that hotel_mode content provider
# while building its UI, and the provider ships inside the b2b packages. Re-enabling
# b2b.hotelmode, b2b.prosettings, b2b.importexport, b2b.rs232csupport and the two b2b.noderuntime
# packages fixed it immediately; the hotel_mode errors went to zero. They are commented out below.
# The Inputs and Wi-Fi buttons were unaffected throughout, so the symptom looks like a dead
# Settings button rather than a broken launcher.
#
# Note for other Sony sets: the settings activity here is
# com.android.tv.settings/com.sony.dtv.settings.MainSettings - the AOSP package with a Sony class.
# The launcher's AOSP fallback (com.android.tv.settings.MainSettings) does not exist on this TV.
