# Notes from this unit (one session on 2026-10-31, verified with screenshots; one day, shorter than
# CONTRIBUTING.md asks):
# - No Wireless debugging on this firmware: USB debugging alone opens port 5555 over Wi-Fi. The TV asks to
#   allow the computer's key once.
# - The saved Wi-Fi belonged to setupwraith (dumpsys wifi showed four entries with
#   cname=com.google.android.tungsten.setupwraith). The owner forgot the network and joined it again in Settings
#   (owner became com.android.tv.settings); setupwraith was then disabled with Wi-Fi and ADB staying up.
# - Casting from a phone still worked after a reboot with setupwraith disabled.
# - Wait about a minute after the last batch before rebooting, as on the other AR2101 lists.
# - com.tcl.smartalexa and com.tcl.usercenter are switched back on at every boot (as on the 40S5400A and
#   32ES570F); both stay idle, so they are commented out below rather than disabled again at each boot.

#   launcherx is enabled again behind "stock_home_redirect" with "key_filter": false; see README.md.
