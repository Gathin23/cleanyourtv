# Inputs & HDMI-CEC:
# Android TV Settings > Device Preferences > Inputs pre-assigns generic labels ("Game console", "Blu-ray")
# to HDMI ports, which hides real device names. Clearing them via `settings put secure tv_input_custom_labels '""'`
# lets CleanYourTV show HDMI-CEC devices natively (e.g. "PlayStation 5", "BD HTS") under the Inputs menu.
#
