# Xiaomi MiTV-AXSO0 (codename dangal; sold as Mi TV 4C Pro 2019 and Mi TV 4A Pro), Android TV 9 (SDK 28),
# firmware PI.6036, Amlogic/droidlogic.
# Live TV app: com.android.tv (set it as live_tv_package and hide it in launcher config.xml).
# Stock launcher: com.google.android.tvlauncher (the default, disabled by build-and-install.sh).
#
# Apply:   TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/disable.sh examples/xiaomi-mitv-axso0/debloat.txt
# Undo:    TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/restore.sh examples/xiaomi-mitv-axso0/debloat.txt
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled. Comment out any line you want to keep.
#
# Notes from this firmware:
# - Default IME is already Gboard, so the Mi TV 4A missing-keyboard quirk doesn't apply.
# - `svc power reboot` gets killed; reboot through adb's own reboot service instead.
# - com.google.android.leanbacklauncher is re-enabled by the firmware at every boot, so it's not listed.
# - Glance (com.glance.tv) puts news and ads on the lock/standby screen; with it off the screensaver is clean.
# - Netflix, Prime Video and YouTube are preinstalled system apps, so they always get BOOT_COMPLETED.
#   Other apps stay out of the boot-time rush after `am force-stop`, until someone opens them again.
# - Power-on can land on Live TV's empty antenna input ("Set up your sources") instead of Home, even with
#   the launcher installed. Xiaomi's `set_auto_play` setting (1 out of the box) does it:
#   `settings put global set_auto_play 0` stops it, and the TV then starts on Home.
#   Undo: `settings put global set_auto_play 1`.
