# Connecting: Developer options has USB debugging only (no Network debugging, no Wireless debugging), and
# USB debugging alone never opened 5555. A USB A-to-A cable from the TV to a Mac worked: the TV enumerates

# the TV's "Select USB configuration" back to Charging; through a USB 3 dock the same cable worked at once.
