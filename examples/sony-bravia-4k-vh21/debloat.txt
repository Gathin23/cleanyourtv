# BRAVIA_VH21_UC-user 12 STT2.230505.001.S108 681961: every package below exists on it except the three
# Australian apps and com.vewd.core.integration.dia. Applied there on 2026-11-10 in three batches: the ACR,
# telemetry, promo, demo, seeds.iot and gestureapp lines, the Sony extras group, and setupwraith. Kept by choice
# there: Assistant, Apple TV, Google TV movies, Play Games, QuickSet and text-to-speech. On Wi-Fi, setupwraith

# Reboot-checked: everything stayed disabled and Home came back to the launcher, after a few seconds of
# Sony's privacy-policy updater (com.sony.dtv.privacypolicy) showing a spinner. Home, Input to HDMI, gear and
# app playback checked on the real remote. A few hours of use so far.
# US models: keep com.vewd.core.service enabled (comment it out below). The US set has the ATSC 3.0 tuner
# (com.sony.dtv.tvinput.atsc3tuner), which uses Vewd: on the XR-65X90L (examples/sony-kd-65x82l) disabling it
# left ATSC 3.0 channels black until it was re-enabled and the TV rebooted. It stayed enabled on the KD-50X85K.

# Launcher: the launcher must target SDK 30+ (it does now) or Play Protect blocks the install on this

# Speed: memory was the bottleneck (swap full, ~480 MB available with the streaming apps open). The last

# owned no saved Wi-Fi network here (check with `dumpsys wifi` before disabling it on Wi-Fi).
