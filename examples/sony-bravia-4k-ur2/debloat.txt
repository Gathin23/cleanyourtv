# Stock launcher: com.google.android.tvlauncher (the restore.sh default is correct).

# Keep the com.sony.dtv.tvinput.* services, com.sony.dtv.braviasync*, com.sony.dtv.tvapi,
# com.sony.dtv.sonysystemservice and the Sony settings apps: they drive HDMI, the tuner, Bravia Sync
# and the remote.
# Already disabled at the factory: com.sony.dtv.b2b.vendorprotocol, com.sony.dtv.b2b.softap,
# screnmirroring.com.

# to its BrowserServiceImpl and crash-loops several times a second without it ("Unable to bind to
# com.vewd.core.service/.BrowserServiceImpl"), which kept the load average near 30. Seen on the third
# unit below; re-enabling Vewd stopped it at once.
#
# Applied on US firmware (dock: Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, ZEE5) and re-confirmed on an EU retail

# YouTube, Spotify). HDMI 1-4/eARC, TVX tuner, Bravia Sync, and remote control verified across reboots.
# Third unit: US UC firmware, same build, on Ethernet, with the Apple TV style launcher as Home (dock: YouTube,
# SportzX, Stremio and a sideloaded app). Lived with 10 days (from 2026-10-20) with the Assistant, Chromecast,
# Miracast, Apple TV and YouTube Music kept on. It also disabled the four commented-out "third unit" lines below,
