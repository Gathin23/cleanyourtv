# Notes from this box:
# - The stock adb on macOS connected fine on port 5555 once USB debugging was on; no wireless-debugging
#   pairing and no tools/tv.py needed.
# - Disabling launcherx alone makes Home resolve to setupwraith's RecoveryActivity; `cmd package
#   set-home-activity` reports Success but does not take effect until setupwraith is disabled too.
# - Right after disabling setupwraith, Wi-Fi dropped for about 20 seconds and the box asked for the Wi-Fi
#   password again. Once it was re-entered, it stayed saved across a reboot.
# - After living with it: Wi-Fi, USB playback (with debugging off), Google Home, the phone remote,
#   Chromecast from a Pixel and Google Assistant all still work. On 737, display is 1920x1080 UI
#   with Settings > Tela e som > Resolução = 4k 60Hz (video 4K, HDR none on Philco 2016 EDID).
# - The remote's shortcut buttons (Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Xiaomi TV+, Apps) all do nothing once
#   launcherx is disabled. Prime Video and Xiaomi TV+ are disabled anyway; Netflix, YouTube and Apps were
#   handled by launcherx, as on the Chromecast with Google TV (see that list for a Key Mapper remap).
#   The launcher brings them back: the Bluetooth remote ("Xiaomi RC", Vendor_2717_Product_32b9.kl) sends
#   Netflix as KEY_VIDEOPHONE -> 193 (BUTTON_6), YouTube as KEY_PRESENTATION -> 190 (BUTTON_3) and Prime
#   Video as KEY_PLAY -> 194 (BUTTON_7). app_keys 193:com.netflix.ninja, 190:<youtube app>,
#   194:<any app> with INPUT_KEY_SERVICE=1 launcher/build-and-install.sh; checked on 737 with the real
#   remote (190 sent to SmartTube, 194 to Nuvio). The remote sleeps after a few seconds and drops the
#   first press that wakes it, so press OK before each button while reading them with getevent -lt.
#
