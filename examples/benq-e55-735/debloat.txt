# Quirks on this firmware, all checked on the TV:
# - USB debugging alone does NOT open port 5555 until the first reboot. Pair once with Wireless
#   debugging instead: `adb mdns services` shows the random pairing and connect ports, then
#   `adb pair <ip>:<pairing port> <code>` and `adb connect <ip>:<connect port>`. After a reboot,
#   with USB debugging still on, plain port 5555 answers.
# - Home keeps going to com.google.android.tungsten.setupwraith/.RecoveryActivity after the stock
#   launcher is disabled: setupwraith must be disabled too (listed below).
# - Standby is MediaTek suspend-to-RAM: once the CPU suspends (seconds after the screen goes off,
#   when no wakelock is held), Bluetooth and Wi-Fi stop. A Chromecast voice remote (Bluetooth only)
#   then cannot turn the TV on; the BenQ IR remote and the power button can. Holding a wakelock
#   to prevent it confuses the vendor standby (panel off, system awake, ARC broken). Wake-on-LAN
#   does work from deep standby: a magic packet to the Wi-Fi MAC brings the TV up in about 5 s.
# - The Chromecast remote's Input button sends KEYCODE_TV, which the stock launcher handled. Its
#   YouTube and Netflix buttons have no key layout on this TV (HID usages 0x0c0077 / 0x0c0078
#   both arrive as KEYCODE_UNKNOWN), so they do nothing once the stock launcher is gone.
