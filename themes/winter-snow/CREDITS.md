# Winter Snow Theme Credits

The wallpapers are original 3D-stylized cartoon winter landscape illustrations created for CleanYourTV and released into the public domain under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal).

- Resolution: 1920x1080 JPEG
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Files in `wallpapers/`:
  - `day.jpg`: Daytime winter cartoon village with blue sky, white clouds, and holiday garlands across the cottages.
  - `night.jpg`: Night mode with deep starry sky, crescent moon, glowing fairy lights, and warm glowing cabin windows and street lanterns.
- Animated snowfall: Live `SnowFallView` minimal snowflakes falling gently across the screen.
