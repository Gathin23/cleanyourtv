The eight wallpapers are photos from Wikimedia Commons, each under its own licence (CC BY or CC BY-SA), not the
repository's MIT licence. `prepare_wallpapers.py` rebuilds them from the originals listed there (it checks each
file's SHA-256). Changes made to every photo: cropped to 16:9 (the box is in the script), resized to 1920x1080,
darkened toward the bottom, the top-left corner and a little under the top-right buttons so the dock, the clock and
the buttons stay readable, and saved again as JPEG without the original's EXIF or other metadata. Adaptations of the
CC BY-SA pictures stay under CC BY-SA. Each picture's credit also shows under the buttons (`wallpaper_captions`).
