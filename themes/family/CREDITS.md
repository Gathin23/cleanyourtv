# family wallpapers

The two line drawings the wallpapers are made from (aile.jpg, the whole family, and aile_V.jpg, the parents alone)
were created by the theme author with Google Gemini, from the author's own written description, with no reference
image; they use no third-party material. The wallpapers lift their lines onto black, recolour them for the
time of day and add a sun or moon drawn in code. Drawings and wallpapers are released under CC0 1.0 (public
domain), so the repository can redistribute them alongside its MIT licence:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- `morning.jpg`: the family with a rising sun, CC0 1.0
- `evening.jpg`: the family with a setting sun, CC0 1.0
- `night.jpg`: the parents under the moon, CC0 1.0
