# Credits

## Artwork

The 24 landscape illustrations in `wallpapers/` were created for this contribution using
OpenAI image generation, then cropped/resized to 1920x1080 and encoded as JPEGs below
300 KiB each. They are AI-generated illustrations, not photographs of identified places.
No third-party photograph or downloaded image is included.

Contributed by [gpetrichenko](https://github.com/gpetrichenko). All 24 wallpaper illustrations are dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is appreciated but not required. Theme configuration and documentation remain under the repository's MIT license.

## Theme configuration

The glass-dock styling builds on `themes/ambient-glass/config.json` by
[v42069](https://github.com/v42069), under the repository's MIT license. The photographs
from ambient-glass are not reused. The seasonal artwork, day/night groups and seven-column
configuration are supplied by this contribution.

## Screenshot

`screenshot.jpg` is captured from an IRBIS 43F1YDX000BS2 running Android 9, with focus on the
dock. Network names, addresses, account information and device identifiers are not displayed.
