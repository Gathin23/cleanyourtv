# Wallpaper credits

All three images are original procedural artwork made for this theme by ADOCR with [make_wallpapers.py](make_wallpapers.py). No photographs, external art, fonts or personal wallpapers are included.

- `wallpapers/01-tidal.jpg`: teal light over navy.
- `wallpapers/02-violet.jpg`: violet and blue light.
- `wallpapers/03-amber.jpg`: amber and violet light.
