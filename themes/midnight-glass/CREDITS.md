# midnight-glass wallpapers

These five pictures are AI-generated (Higgsfield, GPT Image 2.5), not drawn or photographed.

- `wallpapers/01-midnight-cinema.jpg`
- `wallpapers/02-neon-arena.jpg`
- `wallpapers/03-night-orbit.jpg`
- `wallpapers/04-screen-galaxy.jpg`
- `wallpapers/05-concert-lights.jpg`

All five were generated from text prompts by [ruchira-edirisinghe](https://github.com/ruchira-edirisinghe) as
home-screen backdrops for their own TV: a home cinema at night, an esports arena, the Earth at night from orbit, a
wall of glowing screens and a concert crowd. The prompts asked for no text, logos, brand marks or existing
characters, and the pictures were only resized to 1920x1080 afterwards. They are released under CC0 1.0, as far as
the generated pictures carry any rights to release: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
