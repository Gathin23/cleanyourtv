Wallpaper photos from Wikimedia Commons, converted to sRGB where the original carries another colour profile,
cropped to 16:9, resized to 1920x1080 and darkened toward the bottom (the dock), the top-left corner (the clock)
and a little under the top-right buttons. `prepare_wallpapers.py` rebuilds them from the originals:
