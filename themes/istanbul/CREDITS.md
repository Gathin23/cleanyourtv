# istanbul wallpapers

The three pictures are original work by the theme author, drawn entirely in code by `make_wallpapers.py` (Pillow and
NumPy): flat layers of Topkapı Palace, Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, Galata Tower and the Bosphorus Bridge over the
water. They are no photos and use no third-party material, artwork or fonts. Running the script again gives the same
pictures. They are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- `morning.jpg`: Istanbul in the morning, CC0 1.0
- `evening.jpg`: Istanbul at sunset, CC0 1.0
- `night.jpg`: Istanbul at night, CC0 1.0
